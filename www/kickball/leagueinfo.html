Founded in June of 2013, the Rastahorn.com Kickball League brought together people from various backgrounds to compete on common ground: kickball. What began amongst friends as a weekly pick-up game, soon became the focal point of more than 20 people's weeks. With such high demand for more kickball, the league will commence it's first official season in September 2013, expanding upon the players who put left heart, blood and soul on the field all summer. Team Gangstabread and L.L. Kool-Aid will be joined with two expansion teams, collecting the next batch of talent in the kickball hotbed of Burlington, Vermont.


The League's unique incorporation of drinking truly creates a challenging, yet fun and competitive game amongst atheletes and friends alike.


The future is bright for Rastaball, and the league looks to expand more and more as the seasons come and go. For information on the League, or how to inquire about joining with a team of your own; contact kickball@rastahorn.com
